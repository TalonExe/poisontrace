# Finish a bounded research core

Status: accepted, 2026-11-11. Scope and ticket granularity/blocking edges are approved. Issues #20-#31 are published; implementation remains unrequested.

The original research PDF promises more than the prototype implements, and the user chose a smaller core to keep completion manageable. The target is actual read-only MCP evidence feedback, 15 varied deterministic cases, description poisoning and cross-server shadowing, manifest comparison before exposure and invocation, Gemini/OpenAI support, and repeated three-condition evaluation on at least one selected model. Execution and results analysis use separate pages to preserve readability.

This trades breadth for a completed, assessable experiment. Additional defenses, mandatory cross-model research, analyst decision approval/editing, response/case changes and formal usability studies become future work. The detailed boundary and acceptance checks live in [SCOPE_REFINEMENT.md](../SCOPE_REFINEMENT.md). The original audit remains evidence against the original PDF; revised research wording must identify the narrower claims. This decision does not establish effectiveness or authorize implementation, paid calls or deployment.
